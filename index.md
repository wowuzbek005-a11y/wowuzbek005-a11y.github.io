# Channel Autopilot

Channel Autopilot is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It helps the owner of the YouTube channel "Wow Uzbek" prepare, upload and manage their own history videos. It is not offered to other people.

Contact: wowuzbek005@gmail.com

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026.

What the app accesses: with your permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to upload videos and thumbnails to your own channel, set titles and descriptions, read and reply to comments on your videos, and read statistics about your videos.

How data is used: this data is used only to run your own channel. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Where data is stored: everything is stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and keeps no copy elsewhere.

Revoking access: you can remove the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
